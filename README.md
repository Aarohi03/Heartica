## What is Heartica?

Most people get their blood test reports and have no idea what the numbers mean.

Is your cholesterol too high? Is your blood pressure dangerous? Are you at risk of a heart attack?

**Heartica answers these questions for you — in simple way.**

Upload your lab reports or enter your values manually, and Heartica will:
- Read your reports automatically
- Calculate your heart disease risk
- Tell you exactly what each value means
- Give you simple steps to improve your health

No medical knowledge needed. Free to use. Takes less than 1 minute.

---

## How to Use Heartica

### Option 1 — Upload Your Lab Reports (Recommended)

1. Open [heartica.vercel.app](https://heartica.vercel.app)
2. Click **"Upload Medical Reports"**
3. Upload your PDF reports — you can upload up to 3:
   - Lipid Profile report (cholesterol test)
   - Blood Sugar report (diabetes test)
   - Blood Pressure report
4. Enter your Age, Sex, Smoking habit, Family History
5. Click **"Analyze Health Metrics"**
6. Your results are ready in about 10 seconds

### Option 2 — Enter Values Manually

If you don't have a PDF, you can type your values directly:

1. Click **"Enter Values Manually"**
2. Follow the 4 simple steps:
   - Step 1: Basic info (age, height, weight, smoking)
   - Step 2: Cholesterol values (from your lipid test)
   - Step 3: Blood sugar values
   - Step 4: Blood pressure reading
3. Click **"Analyze"**
4. Your results are ready

## What You Get

After analysis, Heartica shows you:

**🎯 Your Risk Score**
A clear percentage — Low, Moderate, or High risk of heart disease

**📊 Your Biomarker Report**
All 9 values from your reports with a simple status:
- ✅ Healthy — value is in the safe range
- ⚠️ Needs Attention — slightly above safe range
- 🔴 High Risk — significantly above safe range

The 9 values checked are:
- Total Cholesterol
- LDL (bad cholesterol)
- HDL (good cholesterol)
- Triglycerides
- Systolic Blood Pressure (upper number)
- Diastolic Blood Pressure (lower number)
- Fasting Blood Sugar
- HbA1c (3-month average blood sugar)
- BMI (calculated from your height and weight)

**💡 What This Means For You**
Plain language explanation of what your results indicate

**✅ What To Do Next**
Simple, actionable steps based on your specific values

**🖨️ Export Your Report**
Download or print your complete health summary to share with your doctor

## What Values to Enter

If you are entering values manually and are unsure which number from your report to use:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Total Cholesterol | The number next to "Total Cholesterol" or "S. Cholesterol" |
| LDL | The number next to "LDL" or "LDL-C" or "Bad Cholesterol" |
| HDL | The number next to "HDL" or "HDL-C" or "Good Cholesterol" |
| Triglycerides | The number next to "Triglycerides" or "Sr. Triglycerides" |
| Systolic BP | The **upper** number in your BP reading (e.g. in 120/80, use 120) |
| Diastolic BP | The **lower** number in your BP reading (e.g. in 120/80, use 80) |
| Fasting Glucose | The number next to "FBS" or "Fasting Blood Sugar" or "Fasting Glucose" |
| HbA1c | The number next to "HbA1c" or "Glycated Haemoglobin" |
| Height | Your height in centimetres (e.g. 165) |
| Weight | Your weight in kilograms (e.g. 68) |

**Don't have all values?** That's okay — leave them blank and Heartica will use typical normal values as a placeholder. Your result will still be useful.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Use recent reports** — ideally from the last 6 months
- **Use fasting blood sugar** — not random glucose, not eAG
- **Upload clear PDFs** — scanned or blurry reports may not extract correctly; use Manual Entry in that case
- **Hindi/regional language reports** may not extract automatically — use Manual Entry if your report is not in English
- **All 3 reports together** give the most accurate result — but even 1 report is enough to get started

## Your Privacy

- Your PDF files are **never stored** — they are deleted immediately after reading
- Your health values are saved only to generate your report
- No name, no Aadhaar, no personal identity is ever collected
- You do not need to create an account

## Important Note

Heartica uses AI and a medically validated formula (Framingham Risk Score) to estimate your cardiovascular risk. It was built in consultation with a practising cardiologist.

However, **Heartica is not a doctor.** It cannot diagnose you. Your results are an estimate — not a medical report. Please share your Heartica results with your doctor and follow their advice.

If your risk score is High — please see a doctor as soon as possible. Do not rely on Heartica alone.
